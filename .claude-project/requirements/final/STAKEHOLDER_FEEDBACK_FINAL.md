# Stakeholder feedback for the final prototype (original wording, lightly formatted)

The stakeholder chose **Variation H for everything**. Feedback for the final end-to-end prototype:

1. Use the government logo at https://motj.gov.bd/site-assets/images/logo.png
2. Images currently show factories and workers. Use images of the institutes: the links provided, and publicly available images related to textile institutes.
3. The nav also includes **Academic**. Should Admissions go under Academic or be separate? (Decided: keep Admissions as a separate top-level item.)
4. Like https://investbangladesh.gov.bd/invest-bangladesh-officers shows the responsible people, About should show the responsible persons at Ministry, Division and District level. **Not principals or teachers.**
5. The login shows candidates, students and teachers. Only **students** should be here. **Candidate login comes from the Admissions options.** (Teachers sign in through the Institution Portal.)
6. Statistics: visualise them like the Variation H sections and the IPEMIS screenshots provided. For graphs, use the chart types shown in the screenshots, chosen to suit the focused user.
7. Add **National University-style top-right important nav items**.
8. Make **login more visible** (clear UX to go to login).
9. Make the "Institutional and Government Portals — separate secure addresses, not part of the public sign-in" links **more visible**, so a reviewer can easily find them in the prototype.
10. Content language should follow the tone of government sites: Invest Bangladesh, IPEMIS, the Ministry of Textiles and Jute, and the institutions' own sites.

Source of truth: `Core_Features_Textile_Institution_Management_System.docx` (Phase 1 and 2) and `../Textile_Institution_HRM_License_SOW_v2.pdf`.
