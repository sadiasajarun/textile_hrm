How access works

Your account decides what you see

Each account is assigned a role and a scope in the hierarchy. You see only the data inside your scope.

  1. MinistryAll Bangladesh
  2. DivisionOne of 8 divisions
  3. DistrictOne district
  4. InstitutionOne institution
  5. Teacher / StudentOwn classes or own record

Roles are assigned by the Ministry Super Admin. Changes are recorded in the activity log.
