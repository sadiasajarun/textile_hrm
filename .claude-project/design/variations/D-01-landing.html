Frequently asked questions

Who can sign in to the system?

Only officers, teachers and students with an account created by their authority. Citizens use the public services without signing in.

How do I track my admission application?

Use Track application with the reference number you received after submitting, for example ADM-2026-104233.

Can anyone verify a certificate?

Organizations register and pay before verifying a certificate or license number. Free public lookup is TBD — requires client confirmation.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. Reset instructions are sent to your registered contact (channel TBD).
