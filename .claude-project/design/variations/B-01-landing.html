How information flows

From institution data to Ministry decisions

  1. Institution

    Records students, classes, attendance, finance and assets once.

  2. District

    Sees every institution in its district side by side.

  3. Division

    Compares districts and follows up on exceptions.

  4. Ministry

    Monitors the whole country and decides from live data.
