The three directions

Summary, strengths and weaknesses.

GPublic Service First

IPEMIS structure + BIDA QuickServ service finder

Every screen answers “what can I do here?”. Large friendly type, a photo hero with a service finder overlapping it, IPEMIS-style coloured statistics, and a step-by-step admission journey that shows the candidate → student ID change. Workspaces use a simple top navigation (no sidebar) and open with task cards (“Needs your decision”, “Happening now — take attendance”).

Typography & shape
Public Sans + Hind Siliguri · 17px body · rounded 14px cards
Navigation
Public: 5-item top nav · Internal: horizontal role nav, no sidebar
Dashboard composition
Task cards first → 4–5 figures → exceptions / drill-down list → notices & reports

Strengths

  • Closest to the stakeholder’s IPEMIS + BIDA brief: big type, image hero, services first
  • Easiest for first-time candidates, students and teachers
  • Admission journey and candidate → student lifecycle are explicit
  • Task-first dashboards make “what to do next” obvious for every role

Weaknesses

  • Least dense: Ministry and Principal see fewer rows per screen
  • Top navigation will get crowded once Phase 2 adds sub-modules
  • Less “premium” first impression than H

HInstitutional & Premium

Invest Bangladesh composition and polish

A confident, editorial national-platform look. Full-screen photo slideshow with a statistics strip, serif display headlines (Fraunces / Noto Serif Bengali), an interactive Bangladesh division map for public insights, and an image-led admissions band. Workspaces use a dark ocean sidebar and “briefing” pages led by large figures, maps and charts; teachers mark attendance inline.

Typography & shape
Fraunces + Noto Serif Bengali display · Source Sans 3 + Noto Sans Bengali text · square 4–8px corners
Navigation
Public: transparent header over hero · Internal: dark left sidebar (5–6 items)
Dashboard composition
Briefing header → KPI band → map/chart + exceptions → approvals table → notices & reports

Strengths

  • Strongest Ministry-level presentation and first impression
  • Map-led drill-down is intuitive for national oversight
  • Distinctive product identity — not a generic portal or template
  • Inline work (attendance register, approve/return) keeps users on one screen

Weaknesses

  • Most visual weight: heavier imagery and charts to maintain
  • Serif display style is less conventional for BD government users
  • Slideshow hero hides 2 of 3 messages at any time

IFunctional Government Workspace

Familiar EMIS / NU portal structure, cleaned up

Looks like the government portals users already know, but tidier: photo banner beside a notice board, a “Latest” ticker, compact stats strip, quick-service lists, a certificate check form and a notice table. Workspaces use a light grouped sidebar (collapsible sub-modules), filter bars, compact KPI strips and work-queue tables with tabs and bulk approval.

Typography & shape
IBM Plex Sans + Noto Sans Bengali · 15px body · 6px corners, borders over shadows
Navigation
Public: search header + ocean nav bar · Internal: top bar + grouped collapsible sidebar
Dashboard composition
Filter bar → KPI strip → tabbed work queue (bulk actions) → division tree table → side notices/reports

Strengths

  • Highest information density — best for Ministry, Principal and data-entry work
  • Most familiar to existing government staff (low training cost)
  • Scales to the full Phase 2 module list without redesign
  • Tables, filters and bulk actions map directly to real workflows

Weaknesses

  • Public site feels closest to “old portal” — weakest emotional impact
  • Smaller type; less friendly for candidates on phones
  • Dense tables need careful mobile treatment

Recommendation

Strongest single direction: G — Public Service First

G fits the brief most closely: a real public website in the IPEMIS spirit, services first (BIDA), the clearest login/portal logic, and dashboards that open with the user’s next action. It is the safest choice for citizens, candidates, students and teachers.

Recommended hybrid

  • Public website: G, with H’s photo quality and its interactive division map in the “Public dashboard” section.
  • Ministry, Division, District and Principal portals: I’s workspace (grouped sidebar, filter bar, work-queue tables, bulk approval, division tree), opened with G’s “Needs your decision” task cards and H’s map for national drill-down.
  • Teacher, Student and Candidate: G’s workspace (top navigation, task-first home, large type), with H’s inline attendance register.

How to decide

Reply with one of: G, H, I, the recommended hybrid, or your own mix (e.g. G public + H Ministry dashboard + I internal workflows).
