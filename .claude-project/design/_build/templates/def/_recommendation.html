Recommended: D · National Service Portal. It reads as an official Bangladesh government service from the first screen, which is what citizens, institution staff and Ministry officers will expect. The public home leads with the four tasks people come for. The same calm shell (green band, top navigation, scope bar) carries through every role without a sidebar to learn. It also has the fewest risks: plain tables and tinted status tags hold up in Bangla, in high-contrast mode and on low-end phones.

  • E · Connected Institution Network has the clearest Ministry story. The health map and the National → Division → District → Institution drill-down answer the monitoring question best. It is heavier for students and teachers, and the map matters less on a phone.
  • F · Action & Service Hub is fastest for daily operators (Principal, Teacher, Student) because every home is a queue of decisions. Its public site feels less like a government portal.
