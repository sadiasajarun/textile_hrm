# Image sources — final prototype

All images were downloaded on 15 Oct 2026 and are stored locally; nothing is hotlinked. Photographs were resized (max 1600 px, JPEG) and not otherwise edited.

Images from official Government of Bangladesh websites are used **for prototype review only**. Before production, the Ministry or Department of Textiles should confirm reuse rights or supply its own photography.
